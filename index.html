What is EOTC?

Education Outdoors New Zealand (EONZ) is the leading national organisation championing outdoor education and Education Outside the Classroom (EOTC) in Aotearoa. We are dedicated to nurturing meaningful connections between educators, learners, and te taiao | the natural world through safe, empowering, and enriching learning experiences. As part of the Year 10 programme students will be participating in four days of Education Outside the Classroom (EOTC). The focus of this programme is one of citizenship and social awareness.

The four days of activities in which your son/daughter will participate are as follows:

• A 3 nights stay at Wainuiomata Camp
• A 3-to-5 hour long walk though through the Rangituhi Trail
• A trip to Adrenalin Forest in Paremata
